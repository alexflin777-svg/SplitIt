## Full description (≤ 4000)

```
SplitIT keeps shared money simple. Add what everyone paid during a trip, a dinner, a flat share or a party, and SplitIT shows who owes whom — with the smallest possible number of transfers.

WHY SPLITIT
• Fair to the cent. Every split adds up exactly to the total — no lost pennies, no rounding arguments.
• Split your way. Equally, or item by item from a receipt: tick who had what, and tips, service and discounts are shared proportionally.
• Scan receipts on your phone. Take a photo and SplitIT reads the items, total and currency right on your device. The photo is not uploaded anywhere.
• Many currencies. Record an expense in the currency you paid in; SplitIT converts it to the event currency and shows the rate it used.
• Guests welcome. Add people who don't have the app yet and keep their share in the same event. Anyone in the event can record a transfer for a guest.
• Settle up. See the minimum set of transfers, mark payments as done and watch balances reach zero.
• Close the event. When everything is settled, close the event: the totals are frozen and nobody can change them by accident.
• Shared in real time. Invite friends with a link; everyone sees the same expenses and balances.
• Export. Share a clear report of the event.

PRIVACY FIRST
• No ads. We do not sell your data.
• Your contact list is read on your device only, and only if you allow it, to fill in a friend's name.
• You can delete your account and data at any time in Profile → Delete account, or at https://www.splitit-apps.com/delete-account

SplitIT is a calculator and a record of shared expenses. It does not move money and does not store payment details — you pay each other the way you usually do.

Available in English, Russian, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese, Turkish, Japanese and Chinese.

This is a beta. We'd love your feedback: info@splitit-apps.com
```
